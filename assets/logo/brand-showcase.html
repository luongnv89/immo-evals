The Concept

A home, verified.

The mark is a house silhouette holding a green checkmark — the product in one glyph: a property listing, independently checked and approved before you commit.

The ink outline keeps it editorial and trustworthy; the leaf-green check is the single accent, the same green used across every report verdict. The two shapes stay visually distinct so the mark still reads as "house + verified" down to a 16px favicon.
